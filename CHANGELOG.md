# 1.0.0
- Party and per-spell damage, DPS and damage shares with native spell icons and names.
- Choose rolling DPS or the game's own spell DPS in the in-game settings.
- Separate boss statistics with configurable retention after defeat.
- A compact, automatically sized meter with native ornamental artwork and a continuous frame.
- Movable meter and settings windows, configurable growth directions, and full spell names, initials or icons only.
- Host-synchronized statistics for multiplayer sessions and results-screen display.

# 0.1.10
- Switch player and spell DPS in F5 between the default rolling interval and the game's native values.
- Native spell DPS uses damage since the spell was equipped divided by its elapsed game time; player DPS sums equipped spells.
- Each viewer chooses their own mode. Boss DPS keeps its rolling interval and freezes after defeat.

# 0.1.9
- A single continuous outer frame and background, with a transparent ornamental divider between boss and party statistics.
- The divider retains its side curls without adding a second stone border.

# 0.1.8
- Defeated boss totals stay visible for 10 seconds by default, then the boss section and divider hide.
- An editable in-game retention field with +/- controls sets 0–3600 seconds independently for each viewer.
- The countdown continues while paused or viewing results, and new encounters start a fresh countdown.
- Completed lower arcs on both ornamental divider ends.

# 0.1.7
- A full-width ornamental card border separates boss statistics from the party totals.
- The divider follows scrolling, fits the automatic layout, and appears only with the boss section.

# 0.1.6
- Native dark card artwork and subdued ornamental lines loaded directly from the game.
- The upper border mirrors the lower border, with matching loops and curves and no emblem or upper arrows.
- Content padding keeps the original ornaments clear of the table text.

# 0.1.5
- Card-style stone frames with luminous double lines and ornamental corner loops for the meter and settings.
- A continuous top edge without a central emblem; corner ornaments retain their proportions as the window grows.

# 0.1.4

- Move the settings window by dragging its title, with its position saved between launches.
- Centered column headings, compact English DMG and Russian УВС labels.
- Extra left spacing for abbreviated and icon-only spell rows.

# 0.1.3

# 0.1.2
